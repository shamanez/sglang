mechanism
  • Two-stage: channel-wise RTN quantization of the runtime tensor to INT8, then H.265/HEVC encoding to a fractional target bitrate (3.5 bits/value for the inter-stage activations).
  • Hardware video codec repurposed as tensor codec: NVENC/NVDEC on NVIDIA GPUs; each tensor is partitioned into chunks, each fed in as an 8-bit Luma-only frame.
  • Codec stages that do the work: intra-frame prediction, DCT transform-quantization, CTU partitioning, CABAC entropy coding; inter-frame/motion prediction disabled as ineffective for tensors.
  • Fractional and variable bit-width (not restricted to integer bits), with CTU-partition-level mixed precision inside one tensor.
  • Dynamic blockwise quantization (Dettmers et al., 2022b) applied to hidden states before pipeline-parallel communication to send less data between stages
  • 8-bit mixed matrix decomposition (LLM.int8()): hidden states and weights split into ~0.1% 16-bit outlier values and 99.9% 8-bit regular values
  • Lossless byte-split (byte-lane) layout transform on serialized FP16 activations, then ZSTD entropy coding; explicitly no quantization
  • New bit-separation: high byte = sign + 5-bit exponent + top 2 mantissa bits; low byte = low 8 mantissa bits (vs ZipNN's exponent/mantissa split)
  • Serializes into a stream buffer then applies ZSTD entropy coding, but with the new bit-separation instead of a raw or exponent/mantissa stream
  • Learned-classifier pruning of draft-tree candidate token states before the internet hop (token-dimension sparsification of the transmitted payload)
  • Padding-free packing: retained candidate states packed into one contiguous tensor plus a small offset array, removing padding from the payload
re-verified ↑
  • 2D FFT of the whole first-layer activation matrix (tokens x hidden size) into the frequency domain; a spectral transform, not a quantizer
  • Low-frequency coefficient retention: keep only the top-left K_S x K_D block of the 2D spectrum and discard the rest (low-pass truncation)
  • Conjugate-symmetry-aware reconstruction: zero-pad the received block back to S x D and apply a 2D IFFT on the server
  • Layer-aware split-point selection: compress only at the first Transformer layer, claimed optimal from early-layer spectral sparsity/smoothness
  • No quantization or entropy coding of the kept coefficients: the K_S x K_D block is transmitted as complex numbers

2 · Findings

Where the compression actually sits. The table's ten columns divide unevenly around the question's centre. Only two records put a compressed activation on a genuinely multi-device pipeline hop during autoregressive serving, and both target slow links, not datacenter interconnects: VcLLM/LLM.265 (4-device pipeline, inter-stage activations at ~3.5 bits/value through hardware video codecs) and Distributed Inference over the Internet (geo-distributed stages, dynamic blockwise quantization — though its activation-path evidence is one implementation sentence, its quality claim is a citation to earlier work, and its client-side activation cache strains the survey's own clause that the tensor not be retained across decode steps; no agent adjudicated that objection). BloomBee compresses the pipeline hop losslessly and its planner switches the codec off whenever bandwidth is high. Three further columns carry pp_stage_boundary only because the topology enum offered nothing else: FourierCompress, Adaptive Split Computing, and the Rate–Distortion study are all single-split edge/cloud or device/server systems, and verifiers confirmed two of them never use parallelism vocabulary at all. The rest of the set sits on a TP collective, an SP boundary, or a single device. Nothing anywhere in the set measures a compressed inter-stage activation path in a conventional multi-GPU, single-datacenter serving deployment — the narrow IN-PP centre of this survey is currently occupied by edge-offloading systems and one video-codec repurposing.

Error accumulation across decode steps is not measured — by anyone. The table's first row is uniform: not_measured in nine of ten records, partial in one (Adaptive Split Computing, a single 50-token horizon with compression active — no drift curve). Every quality number in all ~59 verified operating points is teacher-forced perplexity or likelihood-scored multiple-choice accuracy; verifiers grep-confirmed the absence of any decode-horizon sweep in seven records. Both learned codecs are evaluated off the decode path entirely — ASTRA compresses prefill only while decode runs uncompressed on one device, and the Rate–Distortion study is teacher-forced over fixed 1,024-token windows. Even the set's perturbation-tolerance representative, Massive Activations, scores tolerance by teacher-forced metrics; its own verifier lodged the objection that the "during autoregressive generation" clause is unmet. The strongest reading of the table is therefore negative and load-bearing: the field currently reports that compressed activations are tolerable per forward pass, and has simply never asked whether the error compounds token over token.

The other gaps are equally sharp. Error compensation is "none" in all ten records — IN-ERRCOMP has zero surviving instances, and the one candidate admitted specifically for it died at the BR-ARCH gate (ViT scene classification wearing "LLM" in its title). Low-rank projection, named explicitly in the scope and in a scout angle, never appears: no mechanism is a rank truncation and the rank branch of ratio_or_bits_or_rank is never used. The single BR-W8A8 representative, LLM.int8(), does not in fact deliver the clause that admitted it — no ablation varies activation bits with weights held fixed, per its own reader's misfit note — so the modern activation-quantization literature (ten scout-confirmed admits cut by the cap) remains unrepresented rather than refuted. And where efficiency is measured, it is topology-bound: quantized TP collectives turn into net slowdowns on fast interconnects (0.56–0.88× on A100s), and BloomBee's codec is disabled by its own planner above low-bandwidth regimes — compression pays only where the link is slow, which is precisely where none of the accumulation questions have been asked.

3 · Schema-misfit report

Testing the schema against a small set was the stated purpose of this pass, and the instrument worked: every reader filed a substantive misfit, and the misfits cluster. The most consequential is compressor_provenance — the enum has no token for "hyperparameter hand-tuned or chosen on the evaluation sets", and that single gap manufactured two of the run's three contradicted verdicts (FourierCompress and Adaptive Split Computing were forced to over-claim fixed_analytic or calibrated_offline, and both contradictions were upheld on escalation). Second, the topology enum lacks any edge/cloud split-inference value, so five records map a device/server split onto pp_stage_boundary — a systematic overstatement of pipeline-parallel coverage that the prose above has to walk back by hand. Third, compression_at_generation_time conflates three questions (applied at inference? at every site? ever exercised under decode?), which matters in a survey whose central field is exactly the third question. Beyond those: single-valued fields flatten multi-valued findings (three measured wins, four failure modes, two irreconcilable headline ratios); nothing attributes a bundled result to the activation path alone, which is the normal case, not the exception; lossless and diagnostic mechanisms break the schema's presupposition of a lossy rate-quality knob; mixed-architecture papers are recorded as pure LLM rows because precedent_non_llm is one per-paper boolean; the binary exclusion path destroys quantitative evidence on the way out; and the no-bare-numbers locator rule cannot cite evidence that exists only as table cells or figure bars. The verbatim per-reader misfits and the critic's nine aggregated themes follow.

4.5 · Completeness critic — underserved angles

“inference-time activation quantization **with explicit activation-side analysis** (`BR-W8A8`)”

Served by exactly one slot of twelve (arxiv:2208.07339, NeurIPS 2022), and that slot does not deliver the clause that admitted it. The reader's own schema_misfit records that no ablation varies activation bits alone, because mixed-precision decomposition moves outlier activation columns and their matching weight rows to FP16 together and Sec 4.3 credits the vector-wise/row-wise gap to weight precision — i.e. BR-W8A8(b) is unmet — yet the verifier returned scope_objection 'none' and the bundle never escalated. The shortlist rationale itself states that 'ten further scout-confirmed BR-W8A8 admits were cut by the 12-cap', so the angle's entire modern half (per-token/per-channel scaling, rotation, clipping search) is unrepresented, and no record anywhere in the set reports a quality number that varies activation bits with weights held fixed.

“representational-perturbation tolerance of intermediate activations during autoregressive decode”

Zero coverage of the clause that defines the angle. Its one representative, arxiv:2402.17762, is scored only by teacher-forced perplexity and likelihood-based zero-shot accuracy; its own verifier lodged the objection ('IN-PERTURB's during-autoregressive-generation clause is unmet — tolerance is scored by teacher-forced perplexity and likelihood, never under decoding') and confirmed that 'generat*' occurs once in the whole paper, in unrelated background. The only other perturbation probe in the set (arxiv:2511.04002's Fig 4 magnitude clamping) is likewise scored on zero-shot accuracy at a single 50-token horizon. Across all ten extracted records accumulation_over_generation is not_measured nine times and partial once, so nothing in the set measures how a representational perturbation propagates across decode steps.

“hidden-state / intermediate-representation transmission, learned codecs, low-rank projection of activations at serving time”

Two of three sub-clauses are served — transmission by the IN-PP group, learned codecs by arxiv:2601.22002's trained entropy model and arxiv:2505.19342's VQ — but 'low-rank projection' has no instance: no admitted mechanism is a rank truncation or SVD/low-rank projection of the transmitted activation, and the rank branch of ratio_or_bits_or_rank is never filled in any operating point of any record. The nearest artefacts are arxiv:2601.22002's learned 768->24 channel hyper-prior bottleneck (side information, not a rank) and SVD-LLM, which appears only as a defeated baseline inside arxiv:2510.16418. Both learned codecs are also evaluated off the decode path (2505.19342 compresses prefill only, decode runs uncompressed on the single device holding the last token; 2601.22002 is teacher-forced over fixed 1,024-token windows), so the learned-codec sub-clause is never exercised at generation time either.

“pipeline-parallel / inter-stage communication compression at inference”

Highest slot count in the set (2510.16418, 2312.08361, 2407.00467, 2511.04002, 2604.21072, plus the dead 2604.04654) but the thinnest genuine pipeline-parallel coverage. Four records carry topology 'pp_stage_boundary' as a forced mapping over an edge/cloud or device/server split: the 2511.04002 verifier ran the check and found zero hits for any parallelism vocabulary in the paper, and 2601.22002's reader notes pp_stage_boundary is 'a term it never uses'. Only arxiv:2407.00467 (4-device PP over LLaMA-3-70B) and arxiv:2312.08361 (geo-distributed PP stages) are actually multi-node pipeline-parallel, and in 2312.08361 the in-scope mechanism is a single implementation sentence with no in-paper ratio or quality number. No record measures a compressed inter-stage activation path in a conventional single-datacenter multi-GPU serving configuration.

“expert-parallel / MoE all-to-all dispatch-and-combine activation compression at inference”

No scout angle text reaches this region: angle 1 says pipeline-parallel, BR-TPSP admits TP and SP only, and none of the four angles names expert parallelism or all-to-all. The pool's single MoE candidate (arxiv:2508.06024, Switch-Base) was excluded on the autoregressive half of BR-ARCH rather than on its mechanism, so its exclusion carries no information about whether EP dispatch/combine tensors are compressible. Those tensors satisfy all three clauses of §1.1 and are topologically a collective of the same kind BR-TPSP already admits, so this is a structural blind spot in the sweep vocabulary rather than a judged absence.
